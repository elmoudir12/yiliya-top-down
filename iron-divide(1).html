Field manual / 001

FIGHT. EARN. REARM.

01 / HOLD GROUND
Vanguard is your faction. Outnumber the orange Jackal and purple Onyx soldiers in the control zone. Hot-zone soldiers count twice. First to 100 points wins.

02 / BUILD YOUR BALANCE
Eliminations and objective presence pay cash. Spend it on each new life. A basic kit is issued free if you cannot afford one.

03 / COMBINED ARMS
Press F beside a vehicle to enter. Use the rover to cross the 16 km terrain, a tank for explosive fire, or a helicopter to explore from the sky. Press B to buy and place blast cover.

04 / STAY IN THE FIGHT
Reload with R. Resupply and heal at the blue base. Grenades and cannon blasts destroy barricades and containers. Your base has emergency extraction on the tactical map.

99 simulated AI combatants; no online multiplayer. Only the central industrial district is densely detailed. All weapon, soldier, vehicle, and environment models were generated originally in code. Internet is needed to load the rendering library.
