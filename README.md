# Top Down 2D Game

## Opening Scene

*A black screen. Faint ambient wind.*

**Yir (V.O.):** Hey. Wake up.

**Yir (V.O.):** Master's waiting. You know how he gets.

**Yir (V.O.):** Come on... it's time.

*A pause. A deep breath.*

**Yir (V.O.):** Today's the day.

*Fade in — the player's house.*

## Story So Far

The game begins inside the player's house. A friend named **Yir** wakes the player up and brings them to meet their master — a legendary swordsman.

As they step outside, they encounter a townsman's dog. They suspect their friend **Jin** is poisoning the dog, as he usually does, so they ignore it and let it follow.

Making their way through town to the master's house, they meet both the master and their fourth companion. To no one's surprise, the dog starts talking — and it's Jin.
